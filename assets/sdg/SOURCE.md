# SDG icons

The 17 Sustainable Development Goal icons (English, web version) are from the United Nations:
https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/news/communications-material/ (file `E-SDG-Icons-WEB.zip`).

They are used for informational purposes under the UN "Guidelines for the use of the SDG logo, including the colour wheel, and 17 icons" (https://www.un.org/sustainabledevelopment/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/E_SDG_Guidelines_Sep20238.pdf). Each icon is used whole (number, title and pictogram), square and with its original colours; the only change is scaling from 1500 px to 128 px. The bar colours are the official SDG colour definitions from the same guidelines.
